# Commercial Licensing

The Guidelines Knowledge License 1.0 permits commercial organizations to use and adapt this repository internally in their own development processes.

A separate commercial license is required when the Materials or substantial adaptations are supplied outside the organization as part of a commercial offering.

Examples include:

- paid courses, training programs, or certifications;
- consulting deliverables that reproduce or adapt substantial parts of the Materials;
- products, SaaS features, customer-facing AI assistants, or managed services containing the Materials;
- resale, sublicensing, commercial redistribution, or white-label publication;
- externally offered datasets, RAG systems, benchmarks, or model-training corpora; and
- foundation-model or externally offered model training or fine-tuning.

A commercial license can define the scope, organizations or customers covered, duration, attribution, support, update rights, and price.

To request permission, open a GitHub issue in this repository with the title `Commercial license request`. Do not include confidential information in the issue; private contact details can be exchanged afterward.

No commercial rights are granted until both parties agree in writing.
