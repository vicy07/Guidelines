# AI Usage Policy

This document clarifies how the Guidelines Knowledge License 1.0 applies to AI systems.

## Permitted without a separate commercial license

Organizations may use the Materials in internal AI-assisted development processes, including:

- indexing and semantic search;
- internal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG);
- internal assistants for SDLC guidance, architecture reviews, quality standards, onboarding, and Jira workflows;
- summarization and transformation into internal templates or checklists; and
- evaluation of internal development practices.

The system and its outputs must remain restricted to the organization's internal users. Attribution may be provided centrally as described in `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Not permitted without written permission

- training or fine-tuning a foundation model on the Materials;
- using the Materials to improve an externally offered model or AI service;
- exposing substantial portions of the Materials through a public or customer-facing assistant;
- selling access to a RAG system, assistant, dataset, benchmark, or knowledge base built substantially from the Materials; or
- using an AI system to remove attribution, rebrand the Materials, or recreate a competing publication.

## Third-party AI services

Limited excerpts may be processed by a third-party AI service for a permitted internal purpose only when the organization's account settings and the provider's terms prevent the Materials from being used for general model training or unrelated service improvement.

The organization remains responsible for complying with the license and the provider's data-handling terms.
