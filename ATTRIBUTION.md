## Internal organizational use

Internal organizational use requires:

1. a one-time **Organizational use registration** submitted through the repository's GitHub Issue form; and
2. attribution in one central internal location.

Attribution does not need to appear in every internal document, template, workflow, or screen. A visible notice in one central location is sufficient, such as:

- the internal repository README;
- the knowledge-base or policy landing page;
- the internal RAG or AI-tool information page; or
- the documentation portal's acknowledgements page.

The registration requires only the organization's name, the registrant's GitHub identity, a non-confidential high-level description of use, and acceptance of the license. Do not submit confidential or proprietary information.

Retain the copyright and license notice when copying or distributing files.
